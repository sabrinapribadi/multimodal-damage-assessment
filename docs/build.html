Technical walkthrough · radar and optical damage mapping

How the damage-mapping study was built, phase by phase

At a glance
  1. 1 · WhatA deep-learning model that rates building damage from satellite images after earthquakes, fusing optical and radar (SAR) images.
  2. 2 · WhyResponders need damage maps within hours, but clouds, smoke and night block optical images; radar sees through them.
  3. 3 · WhoUsers: disaster responders and damage analysts.
  4. 4 · WhereResults page and technical walkthrough on GitHub Pages; tile dashboard on Streamlit Community Cloud, no model at runtime.
  5. 5 · When it failsGate set first: radar must add ≥ 5 points of macro F1. Never met in six phases; best +2.2 points; adding Morocco gave −9.6.
  6. 6 · HowResNet-18 with an optical-gated radar branch and a Lovász loss, against an optical-only twin on identical tiles.
Skills applied, with proof
  • Computer vision ResNet-18 backbone, gated two-image fusion, Lovász-Softmax loss baseline_model.py ADR-005
  • Remote sensing optical and SAR tiles, tile labels from building footprints ADR-002 ADR-004
  • Data engineering selective download from a 13 GB zip with HTTP range requests download_events.py
  • Experiment design pre-registered ablation gate, negative results reported phase by phase ADR-001 ADR-003
  • MLOps and CI precomputed results for a free dashboard; CI on every push ADR-006 ci.yml
