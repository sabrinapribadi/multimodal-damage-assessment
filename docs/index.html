Does radar make post-earthquake damage maps more accurate?

At a glance
  1. 1 · WhatA deep-learning model that rates building damage from satellite images after earthquakes, fusing optical and radar (SAR) images.
  2. 2 · WhyResponders need damage maps within hours, but clouds, smoke and night block optical images; radar sees through them.
  3. 3 · WhoUsers: disaster responders and damage analysts.Data: the public BRIGHT dataset (Hugging Face): pre-event optical and post-event radar tiles, labelled intact, damaged or destroyed.
  4. 4 · WhereResults page and technical walkthrough on GitHub Pages; tile dashboard on Streamlit Community Cloud, no model at runtime.
  5. 5 · When it failsGate set first: radar must add ≥ 5 points of macro F1. Never met in six phases; best +2.2 points; adding Morocco gave −9.6.No live A/B test: an offline ablation scores the same tiles with and without radar on validation and test splits.
  6. 6 · HowResNet-18 with an optical-gated radar branch and a Lovász loss, against an optical-only twin on identical tiles.Result: macro F1 0.580 vs 0.558 on validation, but optical-only wins on test (0.512 vs 0.454); macro F1 averages the three damage classes.
